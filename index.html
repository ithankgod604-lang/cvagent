Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  CVAgent by Ifeanyi Aghanama

CVAgent ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your first name, last name, email address, target job sector and location. This is stored in your browser's local storage on your device.

CV and Job Data: When you use the CV optimiser, your CV text and job description are sent to Anthropic's API (Claude AI) for processing. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and is not stored on our servers.

Usage Data: We store your application tracker entries, job alerts, achievements, and settings locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Jobs Data: When you browse the jobs board, queries are sent to the Adzuna API to fetch live job listings. No personal data is sent with these requests.

2. How We Use Your Information
  • To provide AI-powered CV optimisation, interview preparation and career coaching services
  • To display personalised job recommendations based on your profile
  • To save your progress, achievements and application history on your device
  • To improve the performance and functionality of the app
3. Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude AI): CV text and job descriptions are processed by Anthropic's API. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to data processed through their API. See anthropic.com/privacy.

Adzuna: Job search queries are sent to Adzuna's API. See adzuna.co.uk/privacy.

Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, Google's authentication service processes your login. See policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Storage & Security

All personal data (account details, CV history, applications, achievements) is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not operate a central database or server that stores your personal information.

Data sent to third-party APIs (Anthropic, Adzuna) is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Erasure — delete your account and all data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Portability — export your data via Settings → Export My Data
  • Rectification — update your personal information via Settings → Profile
  • Objection — object to data processing by contacting us
6. Children

CVAgent is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Cookies

CVAgent does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage for essential app functionality (saving your settings and progress). No advertising or analytics cookies are used.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of CVAgent after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
